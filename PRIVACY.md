# Computer Basics Privacy Policy

Computer Basics is provided by Toolify Ai Studio.

## Learning data

The app stores lesson completion, bookmarks, flashcard review dates, quiz scores, exercise completion, the name you enter for your certificate, and appearance preferences on your device. Computer Basics has no user account, advertising, or analytics. It does not send your learning data to Toolify Ai Studio.

## Practice exercises

Use the fictional details provided in the exercises. Simulated messages, form entries, and payments stay within the exercise. They are never sent to a recipient or payment service.

## Google Play updates

On supported Android installs, Google Play may check for a newer release and display its update prompt. Google Play handles the update according to its own policies. The update check does not use your learning records.

## External services

Optional links open other websites or apps. Those services handle your information under their own privacy policies. If you email support, we receive the address and message you send and use them to respond.

## Removing data

Clear the app’s data or uninstall it to remove the local learning records. In the browser preview, clear site data to reset progress.
